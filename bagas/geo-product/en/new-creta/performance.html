What engine and transmission power the New CRETA?

The New CRETA is powered by a 1,497 cc Smartstream G1.5 MPI engine generating 115 PS at 6,300 rpm and 143.8 Nm of torque, paired with an Intelligent Variable Transmission (IVT) or 6-speed manual.

What Drive Modes are available in the New CRETA?

Available Drive Modes include Eco for optimal fuel efficiency, Normal for everyday driving, Sport for sharper acceleration, and Smart for adaptive response.
