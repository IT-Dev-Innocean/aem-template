What comfort features does the Hyundai IONIQ 6 offer?

It offers a Relaxation Comfort Seat, Dual-zone Full Automatic Air Conditioning, BOSE Premium Sound System, a Leather-upholstered Steering Wheel and a Wireless Charging System.

Does the IONIQ 6 have a power tailgate?

Yes, the IONIQ 6 features a Smart Power Tailgate.

What is distinctive about the IONIQ 6's doors and console?

It has a Button-less Slim Door with Dual Color Ambient Lighting and a Bridge-type Center Console.
