What engine is used in the STARGAZER X?

It uses a Smartstream G1.5 MPI inline 4-cylinder engine (1,497 cc) generating 115 ps at 6,300 rpm with an Intelligent Variable Transmission (IVT).

What trims are available for the STARGAZER X?

Available trims include STARGAZER X 1.5 IVT Style and STARGAZER X 1.5 IVT Prime.
